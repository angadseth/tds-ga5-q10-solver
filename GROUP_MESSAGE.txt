4 galtiyan jo zyadatar logon ke marks kha jaati hain (README mein detail mein hai):

1. Exactly 3 evidence refs — 2 ya 4 nahi. Cover-sheet, archive aur training wale
   refs bilkul mat daalna, woh decoy hain.
2. amountMinor MINOR units mein — EUR 38,721.92 → 3872192, "38721" nahi.
   JPY jaisi 0-decimal currency alag se dhyan se.
3. application/json ko reject karo → 415. Grader jaan-bujh ke ek bilkul valid
   batch ko application/json se bhejta hai; accept kar liya toh media-type mark gaya.
4. Dusre token ka task = 404, 403 nahi. Warna isolation check fail ho jaata hai.
